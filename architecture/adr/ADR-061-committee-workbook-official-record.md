# ADR-061: Committee workbook official record and member attestation (migration 2115)

- **Status:** Proposed (2026-10-17). Linear CDF-254 (child of CDF-197). Implemented in migration 2115 (Rebase, after 2110 and before 2120), pending security review.
- **Owner decision (binding):** Fady's "OWNER DECISION — AMB-201 / MIGRATION 2110 · Committee-Proceeding Workbook Import Approval + Dual Institutional Record and Platform Attestation Model", project chat, 2026-10-17 19:00 UTC. It "supersedes the earlier simple Chair/Deputy approval model": "The committee record must be documented on TWO parallel but linked institutional layers: LAYER 1 — OFFICIAL SIGNED CDF RECORD; LAYER 2 — PLATFORM-NATIVE ELECTRONIC APPROVAL / ATTESTATION … Neither replaces the other." Sections are cited below as §1 to §18.
- **Placement (coordinator ruling, 2026-10-17 19:13 UTC):** the split is accepted. 2110 (ADR-047, CDF-252) stages, resolves, previews and cancels a committee workbook and refuses its submission and decision with `CONFLICT:OFFICIAL_RECORD_REQUIRED`; 2115 builds the two layers ahead of 2120. "If 2115 exceeds ~4 000 lines, the three read models move to a read-model migration with the Section 13 UI." "Every ballot confirmed" is recorded here as a 2120 follow-on, not built.
- **Size (coordinator ruling, 2026-10-17 20:36 UTC):** 2115 came to about 5 400 lines with its read models. "The two read models with their TS types, gateway and service methods, tests and the view half of E6 move to the read-model migration that ships with the Section 13 UI (it takes the next free number at its turn, you own it)." The commands' TS layer stays here. The remaining ~4 950 lines are accepted over the ~4 000 guideline, delivered as separate commits in review order (schema and states, commands, policies and RLS, reconciliation, audit and notices, tests, docs).
- **Ruled defaults (coordinator, 19:13), each SOURCE_REQUIRED and fail closed:** expected signatories are the chair or acting chair and every attending eligible member; accepted signature method is wet-ink signed and scanned (PDF or image; Word refused); the CDF minutes template is recorded as a reference; "signatories visible" is the secretary's declaration, with the limitation recorded below. F3 attendance (2110) is confirmed as a declaration on the record, never meeting attendance.
- **Activator (owner decision, 2026-10-18 09:17 UTC, binding):** Fady chose "Secretary activates" on the coordinator's card (a tap, no words; relayed by the coordinator at 09:18 UTC), the default this migration was built on, so nothing changed. The secretary (the record's importer) activates the deputy chair under DEC-F03 as built in 2030, for one record only, with a reason, an effective period and an audit event; the importer never approves; the chair is notified and can end the activation before approval (§5). DEC-F03 itself is not re-raised.
- **AGENT-09's conditions (adopted as requirements, coordinator relay 19:23 UTC):** E5 (UNAVAILABLE only when the chair attended; the chair's own attestation still gates commit; stays whatever Fady taps), E6 (a conflict subject's view never names why their scope is limited; their decline allows `ATTENDANCE_MISMATCH` only; the table half is here, the view half moves with the read models), E7 (the record row is locked before every revision check), R6 (no version is completed after submission, and completion never bumps the revision), R7 (scan and SHA-256 trust follow the evidence pattern, PRODUCTION_SUBSTITUTION_REQUIRED), R8 (administrators and external accounts excluded from every new read path, with IDOR probes). R9, from AGENT-09's review of the PR head on 2026-10-18: the subject of a paper-noted conflict oversees nothing on the record, whatever their seat.
- **Protocol:** §12, §18–§22, §24–§27, §29, §39, §45, §65, §82, §83, §87.

## Context

During an outage a committee keeps meeting on paper. 2110 lets the secretary bring that meeting back as a BCP-C1 workbook (one offline meeting and the conflicts noted on paper at it), but nothing may make it authoritative until the owner decided who approves it. The owner decided that a reconstructed committee record needs both the final signed CDF minutes and, in the platform, the chair's approval and each attending eligible member's own attestation, reconciled before commit, and that neither layer stands in for the other (§16: "paper signed = platform action completed" and "platform action = signed official document exists" are never assumed).

## Decision

### 1. One record, one meeting, one revision at a time

- `exchange.bcp_committee_record`: one per committee batch (unique), opened by its importer (`api.open_committee_record`) while the batch is STAGED. It carries a **revision** that a return for correction bumps; a decision or attestation counts only on the revision it was given for.
- **One meeting per record (E1, default):** the workbook's single 09_MEETINGS row resolved IMPORT, with every IMPORT 11_CONFLICTS row referencing it. Two meetings are two workbooks; a conflict about a meeting held online is raised online after restoration. Opening the record and submitting it are refused `ONE_MEETING_PER_RECORD` otherwise (checked at both, since a row's resolution can still change while the workbook is STAGED).
- **The roster** (`exchange.bcp_record_roster`): the proceeding's members who would sit in a meeting scheduled now (the `committee.insert_meeting` filter: active, seated, cleared for the case, not set aside at referral), taken when the record opens. The secretary declares from the signed minutes, all lines at once (`api.declare_committee_record_roster`): attendance (ATTENDED, ATTENDED_PART with joined-late or left-early, ABSENT, RECUSED), whether the signature is visible, and which line signed as chair (at most one, and only the CHAIR or DEPUTY_CHAIR seat line: never a secretary or member seat line). Editable only while the record is DRAFT or RETURNED.

### 2. States

- Record: `DRAFT` → `PENDING_CHAIR_APPROVAL` → `PLATFORM_ATTESTATION_IN_PROGRESS` → `FULLY_ATTESTED` → `READY_TO_COMMIT` → `COMMITTED`; side states `RETURNED` (editable, revision bumped), `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED`. §2's document states map as: DRAFT and PENDING_PHYSICAL_SIGNATURES happen on paper (record DRAFT); SIGNED_OFFICIAL_RECORD is the document version's state; UPLOADED_BY_SECRETARY is submission to the chair; AUTHORITATIVE is COMMITTED. §11's `PENDING_APPROVAL_OR_ATTESTATION` is any of PENDING_CHAIR_APPROVAL, PLATFORM_ATTESTATION_IN_PROGRESS and FULLY_ATTESTED.
- Batch: STAGED until the first submission; SUBMITTED (hash frozen) from then on, including through returns (a wrong workbook row means cancelling and staging again); APPROVED (decided by the approving chair or acting chair) at READY_TO_COMMIT; IMPORTED at COMMITTED; REJECTED with the chair's rejection. `api.decide_bcp_import` keeps refusing committee batches (2110); the record commands drive them.
- Line states (§5), derived live, never stored: `INELIGIBLE` (seat ended, case gate fails, external, deactivated), `ABSENT`, `RECUSED`, `NOT_REQUIRED`, else `PENDING` until `ATTESTED` or `DECLINED_WITH_REASON` on the current revision. **`DEEMED_ATTESTED` does not exist.** `NOT_REQUIRED` is the approving chair's or acting chair's line (their approval is their platform act) and every secretary seat line (below). A line's state follows its **seat, not its person**: one person holds one seat in a proceeding and one roster line.

### 3. Layer 1: the signed minutes (§2, §3, §15)

- `exchange.bcp_record_document`: one row per uploaded version, never overwritten or deleted. Captured: document type, version, the version it supersedes, meeting date, template reference, classification, file name, content type, size, declared SHA-256, uploader, upload time, source `OFFICIAL_SIGNED_MINUTES`. The meeting and proceeding come from the record.
- `api.register_committee_record_document`: the importer, record DRAFT or RETURNED. PDF, PNG, JPEG or TIFF only (wet-ink scan, ruled default), 25 MiB at most, a lowercase SHA-256, a meeting date not in the future, a plain file name. The object key `committee-records/<proceeding>/<record>/<document>` is produced here, never taken from input. QUARANTINED.
- `api.complete_committee_record_document`: the uploader, after the BFF stored the binary through the `EvidenceStorage` port and the scan returned CLEAN → STORED. Audit `OFFICIAL_MINUTES_UPLOADED` (version 1) or `OFFICIAL_MINUTES_VERSIONED` (later versions, with the superseded id). **R6:** only while the record is DRAFT or RETURNED (`RECORD_NOT_EDITABLE` otherwise, so a version registered before submission stays QUARANTINED until a return), and completion changes only that version's storage state: no revision and no record state moves.
- `api.mark_committee_minutes_signed`: the importer marks the newest stored version as the final signed official record, only while DRAFT or RETURNED, only when the roster is fully declared, one line signed as chair and every expected signatory is declared visible (§17 test 12). → SIGNED_OFFICIAL_RECORD, and the record points at it. Audit `OFFICIAL_MINUTES_MARKED_SIGNED`.
- `api.open_committee_record_document`: the BFF streams a stored version only after this check, which is audited (`OFFICIAL_MINUTES_OPENED`). Never a quarantined object; never the subject of a paper-noted conflict.
- A trigger allows a version only its two forward steps (QUARANTINED → STORED → SIGNED_OFFICIAL_RECORD) and refuses every other change, delete and truncate, the table owner's included.
- **R7, PRODUCTION_SUBSTITUTION_REQUIRED:** as for evidence (0900, 2050), the BFF computes the SHA-256 and runs the scan before completion, and the database checks the declared hash and refuses anything but CLEAN. Production replaces this with the CDF storage pipeline (Alibaba OSS with WORM retention, KMS, the approved scanner).

### 4. Layer 2: the chair's approval (§3, §4, §7)

- `api.submit_bcp_import` (committee branch replaced): refused `OFFICIAL_RECORD_REQUIRED` without a record or a signed version, `RECORD_NOT_EDITABLE` unless DRAFT or RETURNED, `ONE_MEETING_PER_RECORD`, and the 2110 checks (rows resolved, preview unchanged, dry run). Then reconciliation (section 6): any discrepancy is audited `SIGNATURE_RECONCILIATION_FAILED` (codes only) and returned as `RECONCILIATION_FAILED`, with nothing else changed. Otherwise the batch is SUBMITTED (first time), the record PENDING_CHAIR_APPROVAL, audit `COMMITTEE_RECORD_SUBMITTED`, and the approver is asked.
- **Who approves:** the CHAIR line, attended, eligible now, with no basis to stand aside (derived: VACANT, INELIGIBLE, CONFLICTED, ABSENT, or excluded as importer, preparer, offline recorder, conflict subject or external account) and no acting chair in force; or the DEPUTY_CHAIR line while formally activated for this record (section 5). Never the importer (§7: `SECRETARY / IMPORTER ≠ CHAIR_APPROVER`), never a fallback to the case manager, the GRC Director, an administrator or another secretary (§11).
- `api.decide_committee_record(record, revision, APPROVE | RETURN | REJECT, reason)`: the approver, on the current revision, record PENDING_CHAIR_APPROVAL. APPROVE needs a clean reconciliation, opens the members' attestation (`CHAIR_APPROVED_IMPORTED_RECORD`) and asks each PENDING line. RETURN (reason required) sends it back on a new revision (`CHAIR_RETURNED_IMPORTED_RECORD`); REJECT (reason required) ends the record and the batch (`CHAIR_REJECTED_IMPORTED_RECORD`). Reasons stay in the decision row and the batch, never in the ledger.
- `api.return_committee_record(record, reason)`: the importer returns the record for correction at any point before commit (§7). A new revision; an APPROVED batch goes back to SUBMITTED. Audit `COMMITTEE_RECORD_RETURNED`.

### 5. Acting chair for one record (§4; E5)

- `api.activate_record_acting_chair(record, basis, reason, effective_to)`: the importer, while DRAFT, RETURNED or PENDING_CHAIR_APPROVAL and before any approval on the revision. The basis must match the one the record shows (`BASIS_NOT_SHOWN`); UNAVAILABLE cannot be derived, so it is stated, and only for a chair declared ATTENDED or ATTENDED_PART (`CHAIR_AVAILABLE` otherwise, E5). The appointee is the DEPUTY_CHAIR line, attended, eligible and not excluded (`NO_ELIGIBLE_DEPUTY`), never the activator. One in force at a time (`ALREADY_ACTIVATED`); a period is required and no maximum is invented (SOURCE_REQUIRED). Audit `RECORD_ACTING_CHAIR_ACTIVATED` (basis code and period, no reason); the chair is told, and the deputy is asked when the record already waits.
- `api.end_record_acting_chair(activation, reason)`: the chair it replaced or the importer, before an approval on the revision. Audit `RECORD_ACTING_CHAIR_ENDED`. Never the subject of a paper-noted conflict (R9): they do not see the activation, so a chair replaced because of a conflict about them cannot end that replacement (no conflict's subject decides on it), and could not approve in any case. An activation also lapses at its end, and ends when the chair it replaced attests or declines: their own act shows they are available (audited `RECORD_ACTING_CHAIR_ENDED` with the act), so a later revision goes back to the chair. An approval already given stands.
- The unavailable chair who attended stays a PENDING line: their own attestation still gates commit (E5). The acting chair moves only the approval.
- The deputy never holds a standing right: outside an activation in force they are refused (§17 test 6).

### 6. Layer 2: each member's own attestation (§5, §7, §9; E6)

- `api.attest_committee_record(record, revision, ATTEST | DECLINE, code, reason)` has **no user parameter**: the caller attests their own line, once per revision, while PLATFORM_ATTESTATION_IN_PROGRESS (so never before the chair). Nobody attests for anyone else (§17 tests 3, 15); the tables take no writes from application roles.
- Scope (§9) is recorded on each attestation: FULL (attended), PARTIAL (joined late or left early), OWN_PARTICIPATION (the subject of a paper-noted conflict). A conflict subject never reads the signed file or the workbook's rows, is never told why, and may decline for `ATTENDANCE_MISMATCH` only (E6). ABSENT, RECUSED, INELIGIBLE and NOT_REQUIRED lines cannot attest (§17 tests 7, 8).
- A decline needs a discrepancy code (the five member-raisable codes of §8) and a reason; the reason stays in the row and reaches only the importer and the chair lines. The importer is told (`COMMITTEE_RECORD_RETURNED`).
- **Secretary capacity (§7: "The system must distinguish the two capacities").** NOT_REQUIRED is about the secretary **seat**, not the person. A secretary seat is administrative and never a member capacity (no secretary seat votes, 2030), so a secretary seat line is NOT_REQUIRED and is not an expected member signatory. A member seat line is required and attested in that capacity only. Today one person holds one seat per committee at a time (1870's commands) and one seat in a proceeding (`committee.proceeding_member` unique on proceeding and user), and the roster holds one line per person, so nobody holds both capacities on one record. If a later seat model let the same person hold a secretary seat and an eligible member seat, opening a record would refuse the second line (fail closed) until the roster carries both: the member seat's line would then be required and attested in that capacity only (coordinator reading, 2026-10-17 21:01 UTC). A test pins the constraints and the seat rule.
- Once no required line is pending: a decline leaves the record waiting for a return, with `SIGNATURE_RECONCILIATION_FAILED` audited; otherwise the record is FULLY_ATTESTED and, reconciled, READY_TO_COMMIT (the batch APPROVED by the approver; `WORKBOOK_READY_TO_COMMIT`).

### 7. Reconciliation (§8)

Derived, always current (`exchange.bcp_record_discrepancies`), never stored:

- `MISSING_OFFICIAL_SIGNATURE`: no current version marked signed, or an attending member line without a visible signature.
- `SIGNATURE_MISMATCH`: nobody signed as chair; the deputy signed while the chair could act; the chair signed while absent, recused or conflicted; a visible signature on a line declared absent or undeclared.
- `CONFLICT_SCOPE_MISMATCH`: a recused member shown as a signatory.
- `ATTENDANCE_MISMATCH`: a line not declared.
- `DOCUMENT_VERSION_MISMATCH`: a stored version newer than the signed one (the source is ambiguous, R6: it blocks, never silently replaces); the signed version dated other than the meeting's Riyadh calendar day; the workbook's hash changed.
- `MISSING_PLATFORM_ATTESTATION`: a line still PENDING once the chair has approved.
- Each member's decline on the current revision, with its code.

Run at submission and at approval (refused), after the last required line acts, and again inside the commit.

### 8. Commit (§11, §12)

`api.apply_bcp_import` (committee branch replaced): only READY_TO_COMMIT, and every gate re-checked in the same transaction (the meeting row, the approval on the current revision by someone still eligible and not excluded, no discrepancy including a pending attestation, the workbook not revoked). It then runs 2110's apply path unchanged (R2, R3) and links the record permanently: the batch, the signed version (with every earlier version linked by `supersedes_id`), the meeting created (`meeting_id`), the meeting row, the proceeding, the roster, the approval and the attestations. Audit `COMMITTEE_WORKBOOK_COMMITTED`. **Nothing writes meeting attendance (F3) and no vote is created (§6):** a 12_VOTES row stays UNSUPPORTED.

### 9. Who sees what (§10, §13; R8)

- The record, its roster line and its own attestations: the importer (while still the proceeding's committee importer) and each roster member who passes the case's gate today (`authz.can_view_committee_record`). Nobody else: not the case team, not `BCP_IMPORT_APPROVE` holders, not platform, technical or database administrators by their role, not an external account (§17 test 14).
- The whole roster, every line's state, the decisions, the declines with their reasons, the discrepancies and the activations: the importer and the chair and deputy chair lines (`authz.committee_record_overseer`), never the subject of a paper-noted conflict in any of those places (R9). The activation's reason, a decision's reason and a member's decline can each say why, so the subject reads their own line and their own attestations only, as a member does.
- The signed file and its metadata: the record's readers except the subject of a paper-noted conflict (`authz.can_view_committee_record_document`). A restricted case's gate applies as for any proceeding member (§10, §17 test 13). **Uploading grants nothing:** every check uses existing seats and gates.
- The workbook's rows: 2110's readers, plus, once submitted, whoever may approve the record now (to compare the workbook with the minutes, §13), never the rows about themselves (R4).
- A member reads, at table level, only their own roster line and their own attestations: never another member's line, decline or vote (§13, §17 test 18), and a conflict subject reads nothing that says why their scope is limited (E6, table half).
- **Read models (moved, size ruling of 20:36 UTC):** the role-shaped record view (with capability flags tied to the commands) and "Committee Record Requiring Your Confirmation", with their TS types, gateway and service methods, tests and the view half of E6, ship in the read-model migration that comes with the Section 13 UI. Until then no screen reads these tables; the commands and their checks are complete without them.

### 10. Notices and audit (§14, §39)

- Four mandatory templates on the workbook's page (`BCP_IMPORT`), ids only: `COMMITTEE_RECORD_APPROVAL_REQUESTED`, `COMMITTEE_RECORD_ATTESTATION_REQUESTED`, `COMMITTEE_RECORD_RETURNED`, `COMMITTEE_RECORD_ACTING_CHAIR_ACTIVATED`.
- Audit events, each separate (§14: "Do not merge these into one generic approval event"): `OFFICIAL_MINUTES_UPLOADED`, `OFFICIAL_MINUTES_VERSIONED`, `CHAIR_APPROVED_IMPORTED_RECORD`, `CHAIR_RETURNED_IMPORTED_RECORD`, `MEMBER_ATTESTED_IMPORTED_RECORD`, `MEMBER_DECLINED_IMPORTED_RECORD`, `SIGNATURE_RECONCILIATION_FAILED`, `WORKBOOK_READY_TO_COMMIT`, `COMMITTEE_WORKBOOK_COMMITTED`, plus `COMMITTEE_RECORD_OPENED`, `COMMITTEE_RECORD_ROSTER_DECLARED`, `OFFICIAL_MINUTES_REGISTERED`, `OFFICIAL_MINUTES_MARKED_SIGNED`, `OFFICIAL_MINUTES_OPENED`, `COMMITTEE_RECORD_SUBMITTED`, `CHAIR_REJECTED_IMPORTED_RECORD`, `COMMITTEE_RECORD_RETURNED`, `RECORD_ACTING_CHAIR_ACTIVATED`, `RECORD_ACTING_CHAIR_ENDED`. Never under the case (2110 D4); ids, codes, hashes, dates and counts only.

## The owner's negative tests (§17)

All in `tests/security/bcp-committee-record-2115.spec.ts`, each named by its number: 1, 10, 12 (Layer 1); 2, 3, 14, 15 (nobody acts for another); 4, 5, 9 (both layers needed); 6 (acting chair); 7, 8 (who attests); 11 (reconciliation); 16, 17 (immutability and versioning); 13 (restricted case); 18 (vote secrecy). Also: the full path with its events in order, E1 (at opening and at submission), E5 (including the chair's act ending the activation), E6 (table half), E7 (and a stale-revision attestation refused after a return) (every record command locks the workbook and the record before reading a state or revision, checked on the function source), R6, R8 (unknown ids NOT_FOUND on every command, a member never takes the importer's steps, a former deputy and an external account read nothing), R9 (the subject in the chair's seat and in the deputy chair's seat reads no activation, no decision and no other member's attestation, and cannot end the activation), rejection and cancellation.

## Known limitations and SOURCE_REQUIRED values

- **The platform cannot read ink.** "Signature visible" is the secretary's declaration, confirmed by the chair's approval and each member's attestation. Expected signatories, the accepted signature method and the template are the ruled defaults until the Committee Charter and the CDF minutes template are sourced.
- No maximum period for an acting chair (SOURCE_REQUIRED). Who activates is decided (owner's card, 2026-10-18): the one-record scope is structural (each activation carries its record, and the activation in force is looked up by record), and §17 test 6 and the E5 test pin who activates and the chair's ending it.
- Hosted DEV has no storage credential, so no version completes there and nothing commits (ruled).
- "Every ballot confirmed": when 2120 brings provisional ballots (DEC-AMB-008), a record whose workbook carries votes must also wait for each member's personal confirmation of their ballot (§6). Not built here; 2120 adds the gate to `exchange.bcp_record_ready`.
- Screens (§13, the secretary's six steps, the chair's comparison view, the member's confirmation page) are Phase 12 UX work, together with the read-model migration that serves them (size ruling).

## Consequences

- A committee workbook becomes authoritative only on both layers; no single person (secretary, chair or administrator) can make it so.
- 2110's committee test commits through the record; a paper-noted conflict about a meeting held online is no longer part of a committee workbook.
- Six tables, four notice templates, nineteen audit actions; nothing renamed or removed (DEC-35). No permission or role is granted; nothing is widened before 2160.
- The read models follow in their own migration, so 2115 alone gives the commands no screen; the API is complete and tested without them.

## Production mapping

Tables, triggers and functions move to RDS PostgreSQL unchanged. The minutes binary moves from the prototype storage adapter to Alibaba OSS with WORM retention and KMS, scanned by the approved scanner (PRODUCTION_SUBSTITUTION_REQUIRED). No new service.
