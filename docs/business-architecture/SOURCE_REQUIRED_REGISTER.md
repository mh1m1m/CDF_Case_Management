## The ten conflicting status codes (DEC-F32)

S021, S022, S023, S025, S026, S027, S028, S030, S099 and S100 mean different statuses in the Drive 150-status list and the owner's 140-status list (ADR-035 §4; `config.legacy_status_map.code_conflict`). Per DEC-F32 neither list wins because it appears first. S099 is mapped to LEGAL_HOLD (DEC-F33). For the other nine, the competing definitions are in CRM_STATUS_RATIONALIZATION.md; the authoritative meaning is SOURCE_REQUIRED unless the Q&A or business architecture resolves it. See CON-013.
